## Projects (summary; full details come in the projects round)

- **Project Horizon** (hardware, 2026):
  - **What it is:** his Grade 12 computer engineering capstone with Algasem Zabarah. A 5-inch FPV quadcopter on their own hand-soldered controller and radio protocol: Arduino Nano, NRF24L01, a 7-byte packet with an XOR checksum (start value 0xA5), and a failsafe that cuts throttle after 1.2s.
  - **Video and parts:** ESP32-CAM video with a web heads-up display, Betaflight, custom PCBs from JLCPCB.
  - **Hardest problem:** motor noise disarming it, fixed with the checksum, the failsafe and rerouted wiring.
- **BasilBot:** an AI email agent run from Telegram, in production for a client. It is built on Olivier Loverde's open-source gmail-agent; Zyad added a provider architecture, approval gates and a sender policy. Stack: Node.js, Gmail API, Telegram Bot API, OpenAI, SQLite.
- **SnapStyle:** a closet app for Android (Capacitor) and the web, with an Item Finder, an Outfit Visualizer (Stability AI image-to-image) and manual entry when the AI fails. Uses IndexedDB.
- **Multi-agent system** (name to come): agents that take on large software projects overnight, with human checkpoints. In progress.
- **MediFlow:** an Android app in Java, built with a team, with Firebase sign-in, a calendar, a timer and a home screen.
